About

Before joining Hanyang University, I completed an M.Tech in Data Science at Delhi Technological University and a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, India.

My previous work has focused on efficient deep learning methods for medical image analysis. I am now exploring research directions in character animation, physical simulation, and learning-based motion generation.

Research interests

  • Computer graphics and animation Physics-based character animation, motion modeling, and contact-aware simulation.
  • Machine learning and robotics Learning for embodied systems, human–robot interaction, and intelligent physical agents.
  • Digital twins and applied AI Simulation-driven systems, cyber-physical environments, and efficient deep learning.
